Profile Studio
Profile Studio — sketch a silhouette, get a parametric curve, fly it.
  1. Lay out guides: lines L, distances D, circles C, and known-part boxes R the shape must wrap.
  2. Draw F the upper half of the profile. Ends snap to guides, the axis and other curves.
  3. The stroke becomes evenly spaced dots, is smoothed, then fitted to a parametric curve.
  4. It is mirrored about the x-axis into a closed silhouette → ② Aero sim.
